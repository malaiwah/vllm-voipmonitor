Provenance, invariants and warnings
Advanced — server-side reset (mutates shared state)

The page never needs this. RESET above is a client-side baseline: it costs no round trip, it lets you hold four baselines at once, and it cannot corrupt what the policy loop and every other viewer are reading. The two controls here do change shared server state.

scope: heatmap rebases the endpoint's own cumulative accumulator. Harmless to the policy.
scope: collector zeroes _count_win / _windows_rolled. The policy loop reads exactly those arrays: the next 1–2 intervals score on a zeroed window, the Jaccard router-shift guard holds all proposals, and pin-forced pairs bypass hysteresis and may swap against index order rather than traffic. It is gated server-side behind VLLM_FQ_HEATMAP_ALLOW_COLLECTOR_ZERO=1.

How to read this figure

Magnitude panel (purple). One hue, light→dark, so it degrades to a valid grayscale ramp under any colour-vision deficiency and under photocopying. The value is log2( E · count(l,e) / Σe count(l,e) ) — the expert's share of its layer expressed in multiples of uniform routing, log2, clipped to ±4 (1/16× to 16×). The domain is fixed and is never auto-scaled from the data, so two frames are always comparable. count is a λ-decayed window, not a token count: the values are floats and the window self-forgets on a ~640-step horizon.

K-tier strip (green). Ordinal, one hue, four fixed steps at even L* spacing (K2 lightest → K5 darkest). The mapping is by K value and is never reassigned by frequency, so two runs with different tier vocabularies are still directly comparable. Tier is not folded into the magnitude fill: at 3 px a bivariate hue×lightness cell makes cross-tier magnitude comparison — the only comparison that matters for re-tiering — unanswerable.

Mismatch panel (diverging). rank(heat) − rank(tier) within each layer, both in [0,1], ties averaged. Orange = hot but under-tiered (starved, promote); blue = cold but over-tiered (wasted bits, demote); neutral gray = the tier agrees with the traffic. If the policy assigns K by heat this panel is gray by construction, which is the correct answer. If every cell is the same tier the panel collapses to the heat rank and carries no tier information — the caption says so when it happens.

Compare panel (diverging). E·share_B − E·share_A, linear, neutral gray at zero. Every row sums to zero, so the panel reads literally as traffic moving between experts within a layer. Log-ratio is offered but warned: it treats 0.03×→0.3× as equal to 2.4×→24× and saturates about half the cells.

Sorted order. The strong dark→light gradient a sorted panel shows is the sort, not a finding. The permutation is computed once from a named reference and applied identically to every panel; its hash is printed on the figure and every panel is asserted to carry the same one.
